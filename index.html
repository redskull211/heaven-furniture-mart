RECOGNIZED IN CHATTOGRAM

Chattogram's furniture community agreed

In 2024, Heaven Furniture Mart was awarded 1st Prize at the Chattogram International Furniture Fair — recognition from the region's furniture community and civic authorities for the standard of work coming out of our Agrabad atelier. It is the kind of validation that only comes from years of consistent craft, piece after piece.

"At Heaven Furniture Mart, we believe furniture is more than just function; it is a reflection of lifestyle, taste, and comfort. Every piece we create is designed to bring lasting elegance into the homes of our clients."
